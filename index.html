Field Guide

Common California Waterfowl

Mallard

Most common duck in CA. Males have green head, females are mottled brown.

Wing: Blue speculum with white borders

Northern Pintail

Elegant duck with long neck. Males have chocolate head, white breast.

Wing: Bronze-green speculum with white trailing edge

Canvasback

Large diving duck. Males have reddish head, black breast.

Wing: Gray with no distinct speculum
